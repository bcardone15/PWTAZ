Frequently Asked Questions

Yes! We ensure all tints meet Nevada's legal requirements. Nevada law allows 35% VLT on front side windows and any darkness on rear windows. We'll help you choose the perfect legal shade.

Ceramic tint blocks significantly more heat (up to 65% vs 35%), won't interfere with electronics or GPS, and offers superior clarity. While more expensive, it provides the best performance and comfort.

We recommend waiting 2-5 days for the film to fully cure. This allows the adhesive to properly bond and prevents any peeling or bubbling. We'll provide detailed care instructions after installation.
